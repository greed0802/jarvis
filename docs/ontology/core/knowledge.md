# Object: Knowledge

Category:
Core / Intelligence

Definition

Knowledge represents structured understanding.

It is the collection of facts, rules, relationships, standards, experience, and learned information that allows Jarvis to reason, explain, validate, and assist users.

Knowledge is independent from files and storage.

Knowledge answers one question:

"What does Jarvis know?"

---

Knowledge is NOT

Knowledge is not:

• Files

• PDFs

• Drawings

• Images

• Excel

• Projects

• Storage

• Memory

• AI Models

Those are Resources.

Knowledge may be extracted from them.
